# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Executive Sponsor provides strategic sponsorship and ensures the project has organizational support, funding, and executive alignment. They help resolve escalated decisions and keep the initiative aligned with broader business priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the initiative and secure executive support
- Approve scope trade-offs and business prioritization decisions
- Remove strategic barriers and unblock major dependencies
- Help resolve escalated risks and conflicts across stakeholders
- Confirm the project remains aligned with business goals and outcomes

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Partners with the Project Manager to agree on prioritization, risks, and escalations
- Works with the Product Manager or Business Owner on business value and decision trade-offs
- Aligns with the Technical Lead on delivery constraints, dependencies, and major risks
- Provides direction to project stakeholders without replacing day-to-day project execution

---

## Product Owner / Business Owner

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Owner represents customer and business value. They help define what success looks like and ensure delivery stays aligned to stakeholder needs.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize work based on value and customer needs
- Clarify acceptance criteria and expected outcomes
- Align stakeholders on requirements and trade-offs
- Validate feature usefulness and business readiness
- Coordinate with delivery teams to sequence work effectively

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with the Project Manager on planning, milestones, and communication
- Provides requirements context to Developers and the Technical Lead
- Collaborates with the Business Analyst on feature clarity and backlog refinement
- Delivers business decisions and feedback to support release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect shapes the technical direction and ensures solution decisions support long-term maintainability, quality, and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture decisions
- Identify design constraints, dependencies, and technical risks
- Support estimation and sequencing for delivery work
- Guide implementation quality and engineering standards
- Coordinate technical escalation paths with project leadership

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Project Manager to translate scope into delivery plans and milestones
- Supports Developers with technical decisions and implementation guidance
- Advises the Product Owner on trade-offs between speed, cost, and technical sustainability
- Collaborates with the Quality/Testing Lead to validate technical quality and readiness

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst or Requirements Lead helps ensure the team understands needs, expected outcomes, and the work required to deliver them clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Capture and refine business requirements and workflows
- Clarify scope, assumptions, and acceptance criteria
- Translate stakeholder needs into actionable delivery input
- Support prioritization and decision-making with clear documentation
- Identify gaps, ambiguities, or dependencies early

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Product Owner or Business Owner to define and prioritize needs
- Supports the Project Manager with planning clarity and risk identification
- Provides Developers and the Technical Lead with unambiguous requirements context
- Helps stakeholders and teams stay aligned during planning and review cycles

---

## Delivery Team Lead / Scrum Master / Flow Facilitator

### Role Summary
This role supports team execution by keeping work moving smoothly, reducing impediments, and helping the team operate effectively within the project process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate team rhythm and collaboration
- Help remove impediments blocking delivery
- Support planning, stand-ups, retrospectives, and team coordination
- Encourage process improvement and flow efficiency
- Monitor team health, workload balance, and work quality

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on planning and reporting without replacing project accountability
- Works with Developers to improve execution flow and issue resolution
- Partners with the Product Owner to support priorities and feedback loops
- Helps the Technical Lead and Quality/Testing Lead integrate process improvements into delivery

---

## Quality / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality or Testing Lead ensures the solution meets quality expectations and that release readiness is based on evidence, validation, and risk awareness.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy, validation approach, and test coverage
- Coordinate testing activities and defect management
- Assess release readiness and risk of defects
- Support quality decisions with measurable evidence
- Help teams improve testability and product reliability

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Product Owner to confirm business quality expectations
- Partners with the Project Manager on readiness and risk communication
- Supports the Technical Lead in validating technical quality and testability
- Coordinates with Release/Change Management for production readiness and validation evidence

---

## Release / Change Manager

### Role Summary
The Release or Change Manager coordinates safe deployment and communication so changes are delivered with the right controls and stakeholder readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and operational handoff
- Prepare communication and readiness checks
- Align deployment timing with business and operational needs
- Track change impacts and rollback readiness
- Support post-release validation and issue triage

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on release milestones and dependencies
- Works with the Technical Lead and Quality/Testing Lead on release readiness
- Collaborates with Operations/Service Owner on support readiness and handoff
- Keeps stakeholders informed in alignment with product and project updates

---

## Risk, Compliance, or Security Partner

### Role Summary
This role helps the team identify and manage risk, controls, compliance obligations, and security considerations that affect project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review risk, compliance, and security exposure
- Advise on controls, mitigations, and required approvals
- Identify dependencies that may affect project readiness
- Escalate issues that require leadership attention
- Support decision-making with risk-informed guidance

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Project Manager and Technical Lead on mitigation plans and escalation
- Advises the Product Owner on impact to value, delivery, and readiness
- Supports Quality/Testing Lead and Release/Change Manager with compliance validation
- Helps Executive Sponsors understand strategic risk and governance needs

---

## Operations / Service Owner

### Role Summary
The Operations or Service Owner ensures production readiness, service stability, and ongoing operational support for delivered changes.

### Responsibilities
- Validate operational readiness before release
- Support service continuity, monitoring, and incident response
- Participate in handoff and production support planning
- Identify operational risks or bottlenecks relevant to deployment
- Provide feedback after release to improve future delivery decisions

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Coordinates with the Release/Change Manager on deployment and support readiness
- Helps the Project Manager understand operational dependencies and timing risks
- Partners with the Technical Lead to validate production assumptions
- Provides post-release feedback to the Product Owner and project stakeholders

---

## Key Stakeholder / Subject-Matter Expert

### Role Summary
The Key Stakeholder or Subject-Matter Expert contributes domain knowledge, decision support, and feedback so delivery stays grounded in real-world operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Provide expertise on business context, workflow, and requirements
- Validate assumptions and decision quality
- Share feedback on process, usability, and operational impact
- Contribute to reviews, acceptance checks, and issue resolution

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with the Product Owner and Business Analyst to clarify needs
- Supports the Project Manager in communication and planning decisions
- Advises the Technical Lead and Quality/Testing Lead on expected outcomes and constraints
- Provides subject-matter perspective during release readiness and retrospective review

---

## Role boundaries and interaction model

These personas are not meant to replace the existing project, product, and delivery roles. Instead, they clarify how accountability, decision rights, and support responsibilities interact across the lifecycle of a project.

- The Project Manager coordinates planning, timing, communication, and risk visibility.
- The Product Owner or Business Owner defines value and prioritization.
- The Technical Lead or Architect shapes the solution and technical constraints.
- Developers deliver the implementation.
- Quality/Test and Release/Change roles verify readiness and safe deployment.
- Executive Sponsors, Risk/Security partners, and Operations roles provide governance, risk awareness, and ongoing service alignment.
- Subject-matter experts and stakeholders contribute focused input without taking over formal project accountability.

This structure helps teams avoid confusion about who owns decisions, who provides input, and how responsibilities shift across initiation, planning, execution, risk management, release, and continuous improvement.
